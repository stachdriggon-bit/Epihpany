# EPIPHANY — Orath: Candidate Cities

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Provisional naming proposals; none are established canon yet.  
**Scope:** A first, deliberately small set of settlement candidates for Orath. These should be tested in stories before names, borders, routes, or political roles are finalized.

## Ground rules

- Orath is the eastern continent in the current working map; its exact coastlines, relief, watersheds, and scale remain provisional.
- These are candidate settlements, not a complete list and not a claim that all six must exist.
- Each candidate has a distinct everyday role and conflict. Not every city needs a mystery, unusual phenomenon, or grand historical secret.
- Relative placements are conditional. Do not force the map to fit a name or assume a proposed river, lake, pass, or coastline is already canon.
- Before adding a place, check the shared place registry and [Anchor Locations](ANCHOR-LOCATIONS.md). A candidate may be the same place as an anchor location; do not create duplicates automatically.

## Reusable place registry — check before creating or writing a place

**This file is the reference record for the Orath candidates below.** Before adding a city, writing a story set in one, creating a quest, card, dialogue, map label, or localization entry, search this registry and reuse the existing place ID and facts. Other documents should link to or reference the place ID and add only story-specific details.

## Candidate settlement list

### OR-CITY-01 — Veyrun / فيرون

**Working role:** A terraced highland city whose neighborhoods manage steep slopes, runoff, and shared water channels.

**Everyday life:** Stonecutters, roofers, gardeners, channel cleaners, and water stewards maintain buildings and terraces. Seasonal maintenance days can matter as much as official festivals.

**Story potential:** A blocked channel affects several districts; a repair technique remembered by older workers is dismissed as outdated, then proves useful in a different way.

**Placement condition:** Requires a highland slope and a reliable seasonal or perennial water source. Exact elevation and source remain open.

**Continuity note:** Its identity is water management and hillside craft, not a generic “city in the mountains.” Do not give every Orath settlement the same terraced layout.

### OR-CITY-02 — Darsen / دارسن

**Working role:** A river-junction market where cargo changes boats, carriers, or route before continuing inland.

**Everyday life:** Warehouse keepers, porters, boat crews, weighers, repairers, and traders negotiate storage, delays, and measurement standards. Local reputation often depends on whether a merchant records losses honestly.

**Story potential:** Two ledgers disagree about a shipment—not necessarily because one is forged, but because the cargo was measured at different stages or units.

**Placement condition:** Requires navigable waterways or a river and overland route meeting at a practical transfer point. The river system is not named or fixed.

**Continuity note:** Keep Darsen distinct from Ilmar's Eiraven: Eiraven is a major river-market city; Darsen's defining function is transfer between transport systems at a junction.

### OR-CITY-03 — Velisar / فيليزار

**Working role:** A settlement serving a difficult mountain road, with guides, pack-animal handlers, lodging, repair stalls, and route notices.

**Everyday life:** Travelers compare weather reports, hire local guides, replace worn equipment, and wait for loads to be redistributed when a path becomes unsafe.

**Story potential:** A new road promises faster trade but bypasses families whose livelihoods depend on the old route. The dispute is economic and practical, not simply tradition versus progress.

**Placement condition:** Requires a pass, ridge route, or constrained mountain corridor. Its exact relationship to other settlements remains open.

**Continuity note:** The existing anchor **OR-LOC-01 — The Switchback Gate** already describes a mountain-pass settlement. Velisar may be its eventual name, an associated nearby settlement, or an unnecessary duplicate. Do not treat them as separate until a story or map gives a clear reason.

### OR-CITY-04 — Ambrin / أمبرين

**Working role:** An inland-shore town whose work and local customs respond to a lake with changing water levels.

**Everyday life:** Fishers, reed workers, boat builders, market sellers, and shore farmers adapt to exposed or flooded ground. Old property markers may no longer sit near the water.

**Story potential:** A disputed shoreline boundary is investigated through several kinds of evidence—old paths, building foundations, family records, and remembered flood years. Each source has limits.

**Placement condition:** Requires a lake or broad inland water body with meaningful level variation. The lake's name, cause of variation, and exact location remain undecided.

**Continuity note:** This may be the same place as anchor **OR-LOC-02 — The Low-Water Shore**. Compare their needs before naming them separately; preserve one stable place ID if they are the same.

### OR-CITY-05 — Qadren / قادرن

**Working role:** A dry-plateau caravan stop with a modest metalworking quarter and workshops that repair tools, fittings, and travel equipment.

**Everyday life:** Smiths, caravan leaders, water sellers, animal handlers, and families supplying food and lodging depend on the timing and size of passing caravans. Workshop apprentices learn to judge materials by sound, wear, and repeated use.

**Story potential:** A shortage of a common metal fitting delays several journeys, exposing how much the town relies on a distant supplier. A substitute works, but only for some loads and conditions.

**Placement condition:** Requires relatively dry upland terrain and a viable route between settlements. Do not assume a vast desert or a particular mineral deposit without further geographic support.

**Continuity note:** Its signature is repair and caravan services, not a city built entirely around mines or a single resource.

### OR-CITY-06 — Lethai / ليثاي

**Working role:** An eastern coastal harbor that supplies ships and handles regional trade without needing to be the continent's largest port.

**Everyday life:** Sailmakers, rope-makers, fishers, dock workers, food sellers, and ship chandlers prepare for departures. The harbor's calendar follows weather windows, maintenance, and the arrival of vessels.

**Story potential:** A harbor improvement benefits larger vessels but changes where smaller boats can land. Residents debate who should pay for maintenance and who gets access.

**Placement condition:** Requires a suitable coastline and sheltered landing. The coast, tides, and routes are not fixed; if the eventual map offers no fitting site, revise or discard the candidate.

**Continuity note:** Lethai's role is ship supply and local harbor life. Do not automatically make it Orath's capital, oldest city, or dominant naval power.

## Cross-city rules

1. **Stable IDs beat names.** If a name changes, retain the ID and update this record.
2. **One place, one record.** Historical names, aliases, and districts belong with the same record unless the story establishes they are genuinely separate.
3. **No duplicate city creation.** Search names, aliases, roles, and IDs before inventing a settlement. Similar functions do not automatically make places identical, but overlaps must be checked.
4. **Descriptions are not copy-paste filler.** Other files should reference the place ID and use only details needed for that scene.
5. **Changes must propagate.** If a place changes materially, update this record first, then check the Atlas, anchor locations, stories, quests, character relationships, cards, and translations.
6. **Keep uncertainty visible.** Capital status, political affiliations, exact routes, water systems, and dates remain open until explicitly decided.

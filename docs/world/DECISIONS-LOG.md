# EPIPHANY — Worldbuilding Decision Log

**Version:** 0.1  
**Purpose:** Prevent provisional ideas from silently becoming canon and preserve reasons for future changes.

## Status definitions

- **Accepted:** explicitly approved as current reference.
- **Provisional:** adopted for planning but may change.
- **Open:** not decided.
- **In-world claim:** belief or report from within the setting; not necessarily objective truth.
- **Superseded:** replaced by a later documented decision.

## Geographic open questions

1. Draw a consistent coastline outline with a provisional scale and orientation grid.
2. Finalize exact bay, cape, peninsula, and island geometry.
3. Refine mountain ridgelines, relative heights, and contour lines.
4. Decide the drainage status of Orath's inland basin.
5. Trace the Ilan River and other major rivers from watershed to outlet.
6. Define lakes and their water balances.
7. Fix widths, depths, and navigability of straits and channels.
8. Establish winds, currents, rainfall, and biome borders.
9. Place candidate settlements and routes after physical geography is checked.

## Content open questions

- Specific civilization names, languages, institutions, and historical periods.
- The detailed magic system: access, sources, methods, costs, limits, and the range of non-human life, to be developed through MAGIC-AND-PHENOMENA.md.
- Which proposed mysteries, if any, become story material.
- Character records to import from existing project materials.
- Exact story/Lore/card cross-links after the core canon is developed.

## Update protocol

When a decision changes:
1. Record the new decision and reason.
2. Mark the old entry superseded rather than deleting its history.
3. Update all affected reference files.
4. Check connected stories, maps, characters, cards, and Lore entries.
5. Do not change the main branch unless and until the project owner chooses to merge this branch.

# EPIPHANY — Worldbuilding Decision Log

**Version:** 0.1  
**Purpose:** Prevent provisional ideas from silently becoming canon and preserve reasons for future changes.

## Status definitions

- **Accepted:** explicitly approved as current reference.
- **Provisional:** adopted for planning but may change.
- **Open:** not decided.
- **In-world claim:** belief or report from within the setting; not necessarily objective truth.
- **Superseded:** replaced by a later documented decision.

## WD-036 — Cosmic laws, restrictions, and anomalies

**Status:** Accepted foundational principles; specific causes and mechanisms remain open.

- Cosmic laws may be relevant both to restrictions affecting sorcerers and to anomalies in the world. Anomalies are the observable outcomes of interactions between those laws and the world's conditions.
- Magic remains open-ended rather than organized into a finalized universal taxonomy of sorcerer classes. Magical access is character-specific, and not everyone can use magic.
- Individual restrictions can alter or complicate how a sorcerer's ability operates; adapting to a restriction does not automatically remove it.
- A visible hand symbol appears when a person becomes a sorcerer and remains fixed throughout life. The established restriction categories and symbols are known in-world. The symbol reveals the category, not the full practical implications of the individual's restriction, ability, skill, or mastery.
- Cosmic restrictions remain poorly understood even by the strongest sorcerers. Their relationship to individual restrictions and hand symbols is not established.
- Unknown entities may exist, but their motives, limits, knowledge, and relationship to cosmic laws are open. No entity or single mechanism is assumed to explain every anomaly.
- The cause of any particular anomaly must remain open unless evidence and an explicit decision establish it. For example, the Returning Current could hypothetically be caused by an unknown sorcerer's ability, but this is only a possibility, not canon.
- Preserve the distinction between observed facts, in-world interpretations, hypotheses, and intentionally open questions.

**Reference:** [Cosmic Laws, Magical Restrictions, and Anomalies](COSMIC-LAWS-AND-ANOMALIES.md).

## WD-039 — Individual restrictions, attempted breach, and lifespan cost

**Status:** Provisional model approved for development; historical cause and detailed mechanics remain open.

- The individual restriction limits the sorcerer's ability; it does not simply make the sorcerer weaker.
- Ordinary use within the restriction does not, by itself, consume lifespan.
- Attempting to breach a restriction is different from ordinary use or exerting greater effort. Breaching is extraordinarily difficult, and not every restriction is assumed to behave alike.
- Magical-energy depletion and lifespan loss are distinct; neither should automatically be treated as the other.
- An attempted breach may consume lifespan, but the cost is not automatic for every attempt and no fixed exchange rate is established.
- Depending on the restriction and circumstances, an exceptional breach might be temporary or narrow, become more dangerous if sustained, yield only part of the intended effect, or produce an unexpected result. These are possibilities, not a universal sequence.
- Rare exceptions may exist, or apparent exceptions may reflect incomplete evidence or mistaken interpretation. No single explanation is assumed.
- A possible historical change in sorcery and the emergence of restrictions remains an open hypothesis. Its cause, timing, mechanism, and connection to lifespan cost are not established.
- The possibility that some sorcerers historically lived for centuries or millennia, and that a few may have reached world-threatening levels of power, remains a hypothesis; age does not imply power.
- Tests and story scenes remain non-canonical unless separately approved. Record observations, interpretations, evidence, and unknowns separately.

**Reference:** [Cosmic Laws, Magical Restrictions, and Anomalies](COSMIC-LAWS-AND-ANOMALIES.md).

## WD-040 — Perception and Detection exploration

**Status:** Exploratory note only; not an official branch or taxonomy. Its earlier relationship to the retired branch-first model is superseded by WD-041.

- Record the exploratory model in [Perception and Detection](PERCEPTION-AND-DETECTION.md) so the initial tests and boundaries are not lost.
- Candidate operations include presence sensing, locating, nature analysis, detecting concealed targets, possible detection without an obvious magical trace, and possible reading of a target's condition/details.
- These are not necessarily sequential stages, and no sorcerer is assumed to possess all of them.
- Detection does not automatically imply control, cancellation, removal, overcoming concealment, identification, or full knowledge of the target.
- Detectability may depend on concealment type, target nature, and the interaction between the concealing sorcerer's power and the perceiver's ability. Strength alone is not a guaranteed outcome.
- Inferring past events from magical residue remains an open, untested question.
- Keep details lightweight; revisit them when required by a story, character, or consistency issue. No hypothesis becomes canon without explicit approval.

## WD-041 — Ability-first sorcery foundation

**Status:** Provisional foundational model for development; character-specific mechanics remain open.

- When a person becomes a sorcerer, a basic ability takes shape, a restriction symbol appears on the hand and remains throughout life, and the sorcerer instinctively understands the general nature of the basic ability.
- The sorcerer knows their restriction within the world's established restriction-symbol system. Knowing the restriction does not automatically teach practical mastery of it.
- Understanding the basic ability does not mean knowing every application or extension; these are developed or discovered through practice, skill, and experience.
- Basic abilities may resemble one another. Skill can broaden an ability's possible applications, but does not make every imaginable effect possible; natural limits remain.
- Individual restrictions are distinct from basic abilities and skill. Their effects depend on the type of restriction. A skilled sorcerer may devise ways to use an ability cleverly while respecting the restriction, without thereby removing or breaching it.
- There is no universal rule that lets a sorcerer fully analyze another ability by sight. What can be inferred or perceived depends on the method and abilities involved.
- Exceptional abilities such as copying and perception are to be worked out when the relevant characters are designed. Do not prescribe one universal set of rules for all such abilities.
- The previous fixed branch taxonomy is retired. Do not force abilities into branches; any future grouping must be an optional organizational aid derived from actual examples, not a rule limiting what magic can do.
- The source of a particular ability and the relationship between magic and any separate energy resource remain open. Do not infer a prediction rule from personality, interests, or ancestry.
- Keep this foundation provisional until explicitly approved as final canon. See [Foundational Sorcery Model](MAGIC-FOUNDATIONS.md).

## WD-042 — Adopted sorcery and cosmic-phenomena terminology

**Status:** Accepted naming decision; detailed mechanics remain open.

- **Aelora Energy (طاقة أيلورا)** is the adopted name for the finite magical energy resource used in sorcery. It can be depleted and restored through rest or other means; exact methods, rates, and deeper source remain open. Tools or abilities may affect capacity, reserves, restoration, transfer, or emergency use, but such effects must be defined individually and do not grant a new basic ability by themselves.
- **Aren (آرِن)** means the general use of Aelora Energy for sorcery by those able to use magic.
- **Aren Art (فن آرِن)** means the individual skills, techniques, and methods a sorcerer develops. It is distinct from the energy resource and from the basic ability itself.
- **Numera (نوميرا)** names phenomena connected to cosmic laws. It does not include every supernatural event or every unexplained occurrence by default; a specific connection must be established.
- Keep energy depletion, ordinary fatigue, and lifespan loss associated with an attempted restriction breach conceptually separate.
- These terms are accepted; the source of Aelora Energy, exact recovery mechanics, and the mechanisms and boundaries of individual Numera remain open.

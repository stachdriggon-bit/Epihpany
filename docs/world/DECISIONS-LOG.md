# EPIPHANY — Worldbuilding Decision Log

**Version:** 0.1  
**Purpose:** Prevent provisional ideas from silently becoming canon and preserve reasons for future changes.

## Status definitions

- **Accepted:** explicitly approved as current reference.
- **Provisional:** adopted for planning but may change.
- **Open:** not decided.
- **In-world claim:** belief or report from within the setting; not necessarily objective truth.
- **Superseded:** replaced by a later documented decision.

## Provisional decisions recorded so far

| ID | Decision | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| WD-001 | Use five principal geographic regions named Ilmar, Orath, Sahir, Nirvan, and Azmar. | Provisional | Names and shapes can change; Azmar refers to archipelagos, not necessarily one landmass. |
| WD-002 | Place Nirvan generally north, Ilmar west, Orath east, and Sahir south. | Provisional | Relative layout only; not a scaled map. |
| WD-003 | Develop the geography before finalizing major cities, borders, and historical empires. | Accepted working method | Helps prevent map/history contradictions. |
| WD-004 | Keep the world's ultimate origin unresolved. | Accepted principle | Earliest records are not proof of the world's beginning. |
| WD-005 | Treat phenomena as varied in distribution, scale, danger, and degree of understanding. | Accepted principle | No universal magic zones or single explanation for all phenomena. |
| WD-006 | Keep character-card rarity separate from narrative importance. | Accepted principle | Collecting is intended to be enjoyable without real-money purchases. |
| WD-007 | Use stable IDs for geographic and character records where practical. | Accepted working method | Renaming should not break cross-references. |

## Geographic open questions

1. Final coastline and continent outlines.
2. Map scale and approximate distances.
3. Exact direction and branches of the Orath mountain belts.
4. Exact source, course, tributaries, and mouth of the Ilan River.
5. Drainage status of Orath's inland basins.
6. Final location of Azmar archipelagos and Strait of Passage.
7. Global winds, currents, rainfall, and precise biome borders.
8. Final settlement locations, city names, political borders, and routes.

## Content open questions

- Specific civilization names, languages, institutions, and historical periods.
- Which proposed mysteries, if any, become story material.
- Character records to import from existing project materials.
- Exact story/Lore/card cross-links after the core canon is developed.

## Update protocol

When a decision changes:
1. Record the new decision and reason.
2. Mark the old entry superseded rather than deleting its history.
3. Update all affected reference files.
4. Check connected stories, maps, characters, cards, and Lore entries.
5. Do not change the main branch unless and until the project owner chooses to merge this branch.

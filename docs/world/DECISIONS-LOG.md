# EPIPHANY — Worldbuilding Decision Log

**Version:** 0.1  
**Purpose:** Prevent provisional ideas from silently becoming canon and preserve reasons for future changes.

## Status definitions

- **Accepted:** explicitly approved as current reference.
- **Provisional:** adopted for planning but may change.
- **Open:** not decided.
- **In-world claim:** belief or report from within the setting; not necessarily objective truth.
- **Superseded:** replaced by a later documented decision.

## WD-036 — Cosmic laws, restrictions, and anomalies

**Status:** Accepted foundational principles; specific causes and mechanisms remain open.

- Cosmic laws may be relevant both to restrictions affecting sorcerers and to anomalies in the world. Anomalies are the observable outcomes of interactions between those laws and the world's conditions.
- Magic remains open-ended rather than organized into a finalized universal taxonomy of sorcerer classes. Magical access is character-specific, and not everyone can use magic.
- Individual restrictions can alter or complicate how a sorcerer's ability operates; adapting to a restriction does not automatically remove it.
- A visible hand symbol appears when a person becomes a sorcerer and remains fixed throughout life. It reveals only the category of the restriction, not the exact restriction, powers, skill, or mastery. Category names and number remain open.
- Cosmic restrictions remain poorly understood even by the strongest sorcerers. Their relationship to individual restrictions and hand symbols is not established.
- Unknown entities may exist, but their motives, limits, knowledge, and relationship to cosmic laws are open. No entity or single mechanism is assumed to explain every anomaly.
- The cause of any particular anomaly must remain open unless evidence and an explicit decision establish it. For example, the Returning Current could hypothetically be caused by an unknown sorcerer's ability, but this is only a possibility, not canon.
- Preserve the distinction between observed facts, in-world interpretations, hypotheses, and intentionally open questions.

**Reference:** [Cosmic Laws, Magical Restrictions, and Anomalies](COSMIC-LAWS-AND-ANOMALIES.md).

## WD-039 — Individual restrictions, attempted breach, and lifespan cost

**Status:** Provisional model approved for development; historical cause and detailed mechanics remain open.

- The individual restriction limits the sorcerer's ability; it does not simply make the sorcerer weaker.
- Ordinary use within the restriction does not, by itself, consume lifespan.
- Attempting to breach a restriction is different from ordinary use or exerting greater effort. Breaching is extraordinarily difficult, and not every restriction is assumed to behave alike.
- Magical-energy depletion and lifespan loss are distinct; neither should automatically be treated as the other.
- An attempted breach may consume lifespan, but the cost is not automatic for every attempt and no fixed exchange rate is established.
- Depending on the restriction and circumstances, an exceptional breach might be temporary or narrow, become more dangerous if sustained, yield only part of the intended effect, or produce an unexpected result. These are possibilities, not a universal sequence.
- Rare exceptions may exist, or apparent exceptions may reflect incomplete evidence or mistaken interpretation. No single explanation is assumed.
- A possible historical change in sorcery and the emergence of restrictions remains an open hypothesis. Its cause, timing, mechanism, and connection to lifespan cost are not established.
- The possibility that some sorcerers historically lived for centuries or millennia, and that a few may have reached world-threatening levels of power, remains a hypothesis; age does not imply power.
- Tests and story scenes remain non-canonical unless separately approved. Record observations, interpretations, evidence, and unknowns separately.

**Reference:** [Cosmic Laws, Magical Restrictions, and Anomalies](COSMIC-LAWS-AND-ANOMALIES.md).

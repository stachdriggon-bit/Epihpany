# EPIPHANY — Local History Register

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Working structure with one provisional pilot-location seed; not a finalized historical gazetteer.  
**Purpose:** Build history from particular places before connecting regions or proposing broad world history.

## 1. Method

Each location keeps its stable ID and detailed geographic description in the appropriate source registry. This document records historical change, not a duplicate city description.

For every location, build history from environmental conditions and responses; livelihoods and infrastructure; local institutions and disagreements; changes in routes and land use; records and memory; and outside connections only where a plausible route or mechanism exists.

Do not assume every location has a ruler, empire, founding myth, disaster, or supernatural mystery. Local history may be ordinary, unevenly documented, or incomplete.

## 2. Status and time rules

- **Accepted:** supported by an explicit project decision or accepted reference.
- **Provisional:** a useful draft to test against geography and stories.
- **In-world account:** a remembered or recorded claim, not automatically objective truth.
- **Open:** not yet established.
- Use a date only when supported. Otherwise use a relative sequence or `Unknown`.
- “Earlier / Middle / Later” in the anchor-location document are relative story phases, not shared world eras.
- Keep event occurrence separate from when it was observed, recorded, discovered, or shown to the player.
- Do not resolve the Ambrin–Low-Water Shore relationship through implication or duplicate map entries.

## 4. Seed record: OR-LOC-02 — The Low-Water Shore

**Status:** Provisional location-history seed derived from the existing anchor concept and pilot outline. It does not settle the lake's cause or the relationship with Ambrin.

- **Location ID:** OR-LOC-02
- **Regional registry source:** `ANCHOR-LOCATIONS.md`
- **Current geographic status:** An inland lake settlement in Orath on the working map; precise relief, hydrology, scale, and final position remain subject to geographic review.
- **Historical date convention:** relative sequence only; calendar dates and universal era are Unknown.
- **Environmental history:** The anchor concept describes a shallow, fluctuating lake and old landing steps that can sit above the waterline during low-water periods. The timing, frequency, magnitude, and ultimate cause of changes are not established.
- **Settlement and livelihood history:** The concept includes households, fishers when conditions permit, boat repairers, traders, and possible salt or mineral work only if local geology supports it. The final resource base remains open.
- **Infrastructure and route history:** Old landing steps preserve evidence of former shoreline levels. Do not assume a particular road, harbor network, or regional trade route until map and route records support it.
- **Institutions and community decisions:** The pilot premise involves a practical local decision affected by records of lake level. The responsible institution, decision procedure, and affected groups still need to be specified in the episode drafts.
- **External connections:** None yet established for this history. Add trade, migration, or political influence only when the route or mechanism is recorded.
- **Records and oral traditions:** The pilot plans two local records that appear to differ. Their authors, purposes, observation points, methods, and time coverage must be authored before any interpretation is treated as reliable.
- **Major local events:** The six entries in `EVENT-REGISTRY.md` associated with the provisional *The Two Records at the Low-Water Shore* pilot. Their IDs and episode sequence must be taken from the registry itself; this document does not create substitute event IDs.
- **Character and story links:** Tarin Vell (OR-CHAR-001), Sella Orin (OR-CHAR-002), and the six-episode provisional pilot.
- **Accepted facts:** The anchor and story roadmap use OR-LOC-02 as the pilot's sole place reference. The lake-level discrepancy is a story premise, not accepted objective history.
- **Provisional proposals:** The place has experienced varying lake levels; residents have accumulated records and memories; a new comparable observation may support a practical decision.
- **In-world accounts / disputed claims:** Whether the discrepancy signals ordinary variation, a long-term shift, or differences between records is unresolved until the story's evidence is authored.
- **Open questions:** What supports the lake's water balance; which communities use it; what resources are actually available; who maintains the landing steps; what decision is at stake; how the records were made; when the story happens; and whether Ambrin is the same settlement, a nearby one, or a separate registry entry.
- **Continuity checks:** No exact dates; no supernatural explanation assumed; no world-scale effect assumed; no duplicated town or route created for Ambrin; physical details must fit the final relief, drainage, and climate model.
- **Sources:** `ANCHOR-LOCATIONS.md`; `STORY-ROADMAP.md`; `TIMELINE.md`; `EVENT-REGISTRY.md`; `stories/README.md`; `DECISIONS-LOG.md`.

- **Infrastructure and route history:** The anchor proposes three stone arches and ferry platforms for larger loads. In the earlier phase, the bridge is newly built and ferry operators lose some business; in the middle phase, the crossing becomes dependable; in the later phase, a new road diverts most traffic and the market shrinks.

- **External connections:** News and languages pass through the crossing in the middle phase, but specific regions, routes, and dates are not established.
- **Records and oral traditions:** Conflicting witness memories are a story possibility, not a documented event yet.
- **Major local events:** None registered yet; create event IDs only when a specific episode or historical event is drafted.
- **Accepted facts:** None beyond the stable anchor ID and the general worldbuilding rules.
- **Provisional proposals:** Bridge construction changes the ferry trade; the crossing becomes a meeting point; a later road changes traffic patterns.
- **Open questions:** Which settlement registry entry is associated with this anchor; river name and basin; toll authority; who paid for the bridge; how long the phases last; and which route later diverts traffic.

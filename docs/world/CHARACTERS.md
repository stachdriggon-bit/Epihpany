# EPIPHANY — Character Canon

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Template and continuity rules; no new character is canonized by this document.

## 1. Character record template

Every recurring or collectible character should have:
- Stable character ID.
- Arabic name and English name; aliases and pronunciation if useful.
- Visual reference or precise visual description.
- Age, lifespan, or creature nature.
- Personality, including contradictions and flaws.
- Independent motivation and current goal.
- Strengths, skills, and explicit limitations.
- Backstory and secrets, with spoiler status.
- Relationships and the history behind them.
- Distinctive English phrase and natural Arabic translation.
- First appearance and known appearances.
- Region, era, faction, and story-use tags.
- Knowledge boundaries: what the character knows, suspects, or misunderstands.
- Character development and unresolved threads.

## 2. Character cards

- Rarity is separate from narrative importance, strength, moral worth, and amount of screen time.
- Card art should match the character's established design.
- Upgrades may reveal additional details, memories, relationships, or alternate presentation, but must not silently contradict canon.
- Card unlock conditions should be compatible with a no-real-money-purchases experience.
- A card description is not automatically an objective in-world historical source.

## 3. Continuity checks

Before using a character in a new story, check chronology, location, knowledge, relationships, motivations, and prior choices. A character should not know information they could not plausibly have learned unless the story establishes a reason.

## 4. Provisional story-specific records

The first lore-linked story pilot has separate, provisional character records for **Tarin Vell (OR-CHAR-001)** and **Sella Orin (OR-CHAR-002)** in [Low-Water Shore Pilot Character Records](stories/LOW-WATER-SHORE-CHARACTERS.md). They are defined only for the draft series and are not yet collectible-card characters or globally finalized canon. Check those records before editing the pilot episodes.

## 5. Status

This document establishes the general template and continuity rules. Existing character details from other project files or conversations should be imported and reconciled before being treated as complete canon. Story-specific records may remain provisional while a pilot is under review.

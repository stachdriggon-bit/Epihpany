# EPIPHANY — City Culture Profiles
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Provisional design pack; none of the profiles below independently establishes canon.  
**Purpose:** Turn the 30 approved settlement names and their current working roles into distinct, playable everyday places without inventing a unified regional culture.

## How to use these profiles
- **Registry facts** are limited to the existing settlement name/ID and its current working role. Check the regional city registry for the authoritative wording.
- **Proposals** are scene-building directions, not established customs, institutions, history, population, government, religion, language, or law.
- **Open** means no answer should be inferred. Anchor overlaps remain unresolved unless a source registry or Decision Log resolves them.
- Use these profiles as a menu, not a checklist. A city should feel lived-in through work, leisure, disagreement, and change—not through a mandatory “regional gimmick.”
- Magic is optional in every profile. If a scene uses it, specify who can interact with it and the limits of that interaction. A place is not magical merely because it is in a fantasy world.
- Do not create named characters, exact dates, dynasties, formal organizations, or cross-regional historical links from these prompts alone.

## Ilmar / إيلمار
**Regional lens:** river systems, forest edges, craft, crossings, and the different ways people make a living from connected land and water.

### IL-CITY-01 — Eiraven / إيرافين
- **Registry role:** Major river-market city; possible capital candidate, not designated.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Market routines depend on arrivals from several directions; sellers and buyers may disagree over whether a shipment should be judged by arrival order, freshness, or prior agreement.
- **People to show:** A stallholder who knows customers by habit; a carrier who tracks delays; a learner trying to compare two inconsistent measures.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A delayed cargo forces a market group to decide which promises can still be kept. Let the outcome depend on negotiation and practical knowledge, not a presumed central authority.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A specialist might inspect an unusual property of one cargo item, but ordinary weighing, testimony, and inspection still matter.
- **Open:** Capital status, market rules, governance, population, and exact river access.

### IL-CITY-02 — Westmere / ويستمير
- **Registry role:** Western coastal port and ship-repair town.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Repair work shapes the rhythm of the waterfront: some people want a vessel launched quickly, while others insist on checking a repair before it leaves.
- **People to show:** A careful repairer, a crew member anxious about departure, and a supplier whose material has arrived late.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A small defect is found just before a planned departure. The conflict is between safety, lost time, and the cost of waiting—not simply between a hero and a villain.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A rare craft practitioner may test one specific material response; this does not replace inspection or grant every repairer magical skill.
- **Open:** Harbor scale, ship types, authority over departures, and trade connections.

### IL-CITY-03 — Talwen / تالْوِن
- **Registry role:** Forest-edge town; timber and crafts.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Woodworkers distinguish materials by use, age, grain, and condition; disagreements arise when a buyer values appearance over durability.
- **People to show:** A craft apprentice, a household repairing a useful object, and someone whose livelihood depends on access to forest resources.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A batch of timber behaves differently from what a customer expected. The solution requires comparing samples and asking who stored them, not declaring the forest enchanted.
- **Magic, if chosen:** An individual may sense one narrow property of wood, with uncertainty or range limits recorded in the story.
- **Open:** Forest access, ownership, environmental rules, and the town's relationship to nearby settlements.

### IL-CITY-04 — Ilanford / إيلانفورد
- **Registry role:** River crossing, bridge, ferry, and market.
- **Daily-life proposal:** People who cross often know the ordinary bottlenecks; first-time travelers may not understand why a queue changes with river conditions or cargo.
- **People to show:** A ferry worker, a bridge repairer, a traveler carrying fragile goods, and a local who dislikes being treated as an automatic guide.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** The bridge and ferry offer different trade-offs today. The scene asks characters to communicate constraints and choose a route rather than assume one crossing is always best.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A practitioner might observe a brief anomaly in the current; safe crossing still depends on conditions and judgment.
- **Open:** Bridge history, crossing fees, route ownership, and any overlap with anchor locations.

### IL-CITY-05 — Calder Vale / وادي كالدر
- **Registry role:** Upland valley town with mills and farms.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Work follows water availability, harvest needs, and maintenance schedules; mill users may disagree about whose task should be delayed when flow is low.
- **People to show:** A farmer who plans ahead, a mill worker who notices machinery changes, and a neighbor with a different priority.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A mill slows unexpectedly before an important delivery. Characters compare practical explanations and negotiate a temporary work order.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A particular person may help detect one unusual vibration or water behavior; no universal enchanted mill system is implied.
- **Open:** Water rights, crop choices, mill ownership, and seasonal calendar.

### IL-CITY-06 — Sorell / سوريل
- **Registry role:** Southern route and caravan market.
- **Daily-life proposal:** People balance cargo space, animal or vehicle care, rest, and changing travel conditions. The market serves residents as well as passers-through.
- **People to show:** A route planner, a local vendor tired of being overlooked by travelers, and a carrier with a practical shortcut that is not always safe.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A caravan cannot carry everything promised. Characters decide what to leave, transfer, or renegotiate and explain the choice to affected people.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A specialist might identify a narrow sign of a route anomaly, but route knowledge remains local and fallible.
- **Open:** Exact route links, transport technology, market schedule, and caravan organization.

## Orath / أوراث
**Regional lens:** highland routes, stonework, inland basins, transfers, and the work required to keep passage reliable.

### OR-CITY-01 — Veyrun / فيرون
- **Registry role:** Terraced highland city known for stonework and water management.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Residents coordinate upkeep across stepped streets, channels, and buildings; a repair that helps one terrace may inconvenience another.
- **People to show:** A stoneworker who values long-term stability, a resident who needs immediate access, and someone who records maintenance.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A water channel needs repair while nearby households still need it. The group must agree on a temporary arrangement and a clear return-to-service condition.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A specialist may test one property of stone or water, without making all construction effortless.
- **Open:** Water allocation rules, political status, and whether any formal public works body exists.

### OR-CITY-02 — Darsen / دارسن
- **Registry role:** River-junction market and cargo-transfer town.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Cargo changes hands between routes; labels, quantities, and responsibility can become unclear during a hurried transfer.
- **People to show:** A transfer worker, a merchant who wants speed, and a receiver who insists on checking the inventory.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** One crate appears on two lists, while another is missing. Resolve it through records and conversations before treating it as theft.
- **Magic, if chosen:** An individual might detect a distinctive trace on a single object; evidence still needs corroboration.
- **Open:** Navigability, route directions, market authority, and connections to other settlements.

### OR-CITY-03 — Velisar / فيليزار
- **Registry role:** Mountain-road settlement; may overlap with the Switchback Gate anchor.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Travelers compare road conditions and the limits of their equipment; locals may be frustrated when outsiders treat yesterday's safe route as today's guarantee.
- **People to show:** A road maintainer, a traveler who wants to continue, and a resident responsible for a practical warning.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** Two route notes disagree after a change in conditions. Characters must state when each note was made and what kind of traveler it describes.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A trained person may notice one specific sign of instability, not predict all hazards.
- **Open:** Velisar's identity relationship to the Switchback Gate anchor, exact route geometry, and local authority.

### OR-CITY-04 — Ambrin / أمبرين
- **Registry role:** Inland-shore town shaped by a fluctuating lake.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Residents adjust paths, work, and expectations as the shoreline changes. Different records may be accurate for different dates or purposes.
- **People to show:** A record keeper, a person whose work depends on the waterline, and a visitor who assumes one map must be definitive.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** Two trustworthy records disagree about a shoreline feature. The characters investigate the purpose and timing of each record rather than force a single answer.
- **Magic, if chosen:** An unusual echo or water response may be observed locally, but it is not automatically a complete recording or proof of a cause.
- **Open:** Relationship to OR-LOC-02 — The Low-Water Shore. For the existing pilot, continue using OR-LOC-02 only; do not duplicate places or events under both IDs.

### OR-CITY-05 — Qadren / قادرن
- **Registry role:** Dry-plateau caravan stop and metalworking center.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Repair needs compete with limited supplies and travel deadlines; a skilled craftsperson may refuse a rushed job for sound practical reasons.
- **People to show:** A metalworker who explains trade-offs, a traveler with a damaged tool, and a supplier balancing several customers.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A repair can be made quickly or made to last, but not both with the materials currently available. Characters clarify intended use before choosing.
- **Magic, if chosen:** One craftsperson may work with a narrowly defined unusual property of metal; ordinary heat, wear, and workmanship remain relevant.
- **Open:** Metal sources, fuel, workshop organization, and the actual routes served.

### OR-CITY-06 — Lethai / ليثاي
- **Registry role:** Eastern coastal harbor and ship-supply town.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Supplies are prepared around expected arrivals, but a vessel's needs can differ from its manifest or the assumptions of a seller.
- **People to show:** A provisioning worker, a crew member with a changed itinerary, and a local who depends on the same stock.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** The last of a useful supply is requested by two parties. The scene turns on explaining needs and alternatives, not inventing a universal harbor law.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A rare specialist may test one property of a stored supply, with clear limits.
- **Open:** Harbor conditions, ship routes, supply chains, and governance.

## Tharima / ثاريما
**Regional lens:** seasonal water, market windows, route services, coastal work, and skilled adaptation. Avoid treating all communities as identical or uniformly nomadic.

### TH-CITY-01 — Namaris / ناماريس
- **Registry role:** Inland oasis-town with gardens and a maintained water network.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Garden work and water maintenance create shared dependencies; people may agree that upkeep matters while disagreeing about timing and priority.
- **People to show:** A gardener experimenting with a crop, a maintenance worker, and a household that needs water at a different hour.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A small leak is discovered before a busy day. The group must decide what to repair immediately and how to communicate temporary limits.
- **Magic, if chosen:** An individual may sense one unusual water condition, but the network still needs physical maintenance and accountable decisions.
- **Open:** Water source, allocation, ownership, crop choices, and seasonal patterns.

### TH-CITY-02 — Saffra / سفرا
- **Registry role:** Seasonal wadi market and meeting point; possible anchor overlap.
- **Daily-life proposal:** The market's usefulness depends on timing, reliable information, and the ability to adapt when expected visitors cannot arrive.
- **People to show:** A seller who has prepared perishable goods, a traveler with a changed route, and a local who knows a recent channel condition.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A meeting must be rescheduled because the route is uncertain. Characters distinguish confirmed observations from second-hand reports.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A local practitioner may interpret one unusual sign in the environment, but it does not replace checking conditions.
- **Open:** Possible anchor identity, market calendar, and the wadi's exact behavior.

### TH-CITY-03 — Kharif / خريف
- **Registry role:** Upland settlement built around spring-fed terraces; possible anchor overlap.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Terrace upkeep depends on cooperation across levels; a repair at one point can redirect work or water elsewhere.
- **People to show:** A terrace grower, a repairer, and a younger resident proposing a different maintenance sequence.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A blocked channel creates an argument over which terrace to inspect first. The resolution follows observed flow and agreed needs.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A capable individual may interact with one spring-related phenomenon; do not assume every resident can do so.
- **Open:** Possible anchor identity, spring reliability, and local maintenance arrangements.

### TH-CITY-04 — Rihal / رحال
- **Registry role:** Caravan service town at the meeting of long-distance routes.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Travelers need repairs, rest, information, and honest estimates; residents also need their own work and privacy respected.
- **People to show:** A service worker, a traveler who has misjudged the time needed, and a resident who challenges a careless assumption.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A route recommendation is good for one kind of traveler but unsuitable for another. Characters clarify load, urgency, and conditions before advising.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A specialist might detect one limited anomaly along a route; no infallible route-finding power is implied.
- **Open:** Actual routes, service economy, transport, and any relationship to other route towns.

### TH-CITY-05 — Tazir / تازير
- **Registry role:** Coastal salt-and-fishing town with changing shoreline work.
- **Daily-life proposal:** People combine knowledge of tides or local water conditions with practical work; shoreline change affects tasks unevenly.
- **People to show:** A fisher who values observation, a salt worker managing equipment, and a household adapting to a changed work area.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A familiar work site is less useful than expected. Characters compare recent observations before deciding whether the change is temporary.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A phenomenon may affect one patch of water or material, not the entire coast by default.
- **Open:** Coastline, fishing methods, salt process, and seasonal cycle.

### TH-CITY-06 — Merzun / مرزون
- **Registry role:** Hill-country craft town known for pottery and fired-clay goods.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Makers debate the balance between speed, fuel, consistency, and experimenting with a new form.
- **People to show:** A potter who trusts tested methods, an apprentice who notices a pattern, and a buyer with a specific practical need.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A batch comes out uneven. The group compares clay, drying, firing, and handling before blaming a person or a supernatural cause.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A practitioner may influence one carefully bounded stage or property, but craft knowledge remains essential.
- **Open:** Clay sources, kiln arrangements, trade, and workshop organization.

## Nirvan / نيرفان
**Regional lens:** seasonal access, forests, lakes, exposed passes, and varied northern coasts. Do not imply that every settlement is permanently frozen or culturally uniform.

### NI-CITY-01 — Orven Reach / أورڤن ريتش
- **Registry role:** Sheltered northern harbor and repair stop; possible anchor overlap.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Arrival plans are shaped by local conditions and repair needs; a sheltered harbor can still face shortages or delays.
- **People to show:** A repair worker, a crew member eager to depart, and a local supplier balancing winter stock.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A vessel needs a repair that will delay another job. The group negotiates a realistic schedule and identifies what is safe to postpone.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A trained observer may notice a narrow weather or water anomaly; it is not a guarantee of future conditions.
- **Open:** Possible anchor overlap, harbor geography, and seasonal access.

### NI-CITY-02 — Pellisar / بيليسار
- **Registry role:** Lakeside town with seasonal boat and overland links.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Residents switch between routes as conditions change; advice must account for cargo, mobility, and the traveler's familiarity with the area.
- **People to show:** A boat operator, a guide for an overland route, and a passenger who has packed for the wrong conditions.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A traveler insists on a familiar route while locals recommend an alternative. Each side must explain the evidence behind its view.
- **Magic, if chosen:** One individual might observe an unusual lake pattern, but ordinary route planning remains necessary.
- **Open:** Lake outlet, seasonal conditions, route connections, and anchor relationships.

### NI-CITY-03 — Varnholt / ڤارن هولت
- **Registry role:** Forest-edge timber and winter-supply town.
- **Daily-life proposal:** People prepare materials before access becomes harder; planning disputes emerge when current needs compete with future reserves.
- **People to show:** A storekeeper, a timber worker, and a household questioning how much should be kept in reserve.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A supply estimate proves optimistic. Characters check records and revise plans without assuming deliberate deception.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A forest-related effect may be tied to one practitioner or place-specific condition, not every tree or worker.
- **Open:** Forest ecology, storage methods, access rights, and the length of difficult seasons.

### NI-CITY-04 — Iskell / إسكِل
- **Registry role:** Upland settlement supporting a weather-exposed pass.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Warnings need context: the same condition can affect a light traveler and a loaded carrier differently.
- **People to show:** A pass maintainer, someone eager to cross, and a messenger who must communicate uncertainty accurately.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A warning is technically true but too vague to be useful. Characters improve it by stating observation time, location, and who should take extra care.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A person may recognize one limited sign of unusual weather; no one can guarantee the pass is safe.
- **Open:** Pass route, shelter arrangements, local authority, and exact climate.

### NI-CITY-05 — Mireva / ميريفا
- **Registry role:** Wetland and lake-outlet community.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Work and travel depend on knowing which ground is stable and where water is moving; outsiders may mistake a familiar-looking path for a reliable one.
- **People to show:** A local guide, a worker maintaining a crossing, and a visitor who wants a shorter route.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A path has changed since the last record. Characters verify it before allowing convenience to overrule safety.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A practitioner may sense one property of wetland water or ground, with stated uncertainty.
- **Open:** Outlet geometry, wetland extent, crossing technology, and seasonal shifts.

### NI-CITY-06 — Caerun / كايرون
- **Registry role:** Transitional-belt market linking northern and milder routes.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Goods and habits from different climates meet here, but that does not mean all visitors share a common identity or agree on standards.
- **People to show:** A vendor adjusting a product for different needs, a traveler comparing advice, and a resident who is tired of the town being treated only as a stopover.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** Two buyers use the same word for different product specifications. Clarification prevents a needless dispute.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A specialist might test a specific material or environmental behavior; there is no universal “northern magic.”
- **Open:** Exact regional boundary, route links, and market rules.

## Azmar / أزمار
**Regional lens:** distinct island communities, navigation, repair, supplies, and negotiated dependence. Do not treat the archipelago as one unified state or culture.

### AZ-CITY-01 — Selka Haven / سيلكا هيفن
- **Registry role:** Busy harbor at a junction of island passages; possible anchor overlap.
- **Daily-life proposal:** A harbor's usefulness comes from coordinating different vessels, schedules, supplies, and local information—not from assuming every visitor follows one standard.
- **People to show:** A harbor worker, a crew unfamiliar with local conditions, and a resident who needs the shared facilities for everyday work.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** Two arrivals need the same limited service. Characters explain urgency and constraints before choosing an order.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A specialist may examine one unusual channel signal; it does not replace navigation knowledge.
- **Open:** Possible anchor overlap, authority over passage notices, and the island groups served.

### AZ-CITY-02 — Tern Isle / جزيرة تيرن
- **Registry role:** Small island settlement maintaining navigation markers.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Marker maintenance is routine, skilled work; a marker is useful only when people know what it means and when it was checked.
- **People to show:** A marker keeper, a visiting crew, and a local who challenges an outdated assumption.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A marker appears correct but is unsuitable for one vessel's draft or the current conditions. The crew must explain the mismatch.
- **Magic, if chosen:** One marker might have a limited unusual response; no marker is automatically magical or universally reliable.
- **Open:** Marker conventions, maintenance responsibilities, and route coverage.

### AZ-CITY-03 — Oruven / أوروفن
- **Registry role:** Freshwater-dependent inland settlement on a larger island.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Freshwater storage and use influence planning, but residents still have differing priorities and practical ways to reduce waste.
- **People to show:** Someone responsible for a water store, a grower adjusting a plan, and a visitor who underestimated the island's supply needs.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A delivery is delayed and the stored amount is uncertain. Characters inspect the records and make a transparent short-term plan.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A capable person might interact with one water-related effect; it cannot create unlimited fresh water by assumption.
- **Open:** Water source, storage, population, and supply routes.

### AZ-CITY-04 — Maret Quay / رصيف ماريت
- **Registry role:** Repair and resupply stop for short maritime routes.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Repair schedules must fit vessel needs, available materials, and the next safe departure opportunity.
- **People to show:** A repairer, a supplier with limited stock, and a crew member who must revise a plan.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A needed part is unavailable. Characters decide whether to improvise safely, wait for supplies, or change the journey.
- **Magic, if chosen:** One specialist may test a particular material response; magic does not remove the need to verify a repair.
- **Open:** Repair capabilities, supply links, harbor conditions, and route network.

### AZ-CITY-05 — Vey Shoals / ضفاف ڤي
- **Registry role:** Settlement adapting to shifting shoals and exposed channels.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Local knowledge is valuable but must be dated and tied to conditions; an old safe passage can become misleading.
- **People to show:** A navigator who records observations, a resident whose work area has shifted, and a visitor who trusts a copied chart too much.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** Two route descriptions disagree. The characters compare dates, vessel types, and the conditions each record covers.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A localized anomaly may be one possible explanation, but changing water and incomplete evidence remain live alternatives.
- **Open:** Shoal dynamics, marker systems, route authority, and safe passage conditions.

### AZ-CITY-06 — Far Lantern / الفانوس البعيد
- **Registry role:** Remote out-island community with irregular connections.
- **Daily-life proposal:** Residents plan around uncertain deliveries and must decide what knowledge to share with visitors; remoteness does not mean ignorance or uniform isolation.
- **People to show:** A person maintaining a useful reserve, a visitor bringing unexpected news, and a resident with reasons to question it.
- **Local tension / scene seed:** A delivery fails to arrive, but the cause is unknown. The community weighs immediate needs while keeping multiple explanations open.
- **Magic, if chosen:** A rare local phenomenon may affect one observation or task; do not make it the sole explanation for every difficulty.
- **Open:** Freshwater, supply cadence, communication methods, and which routes connect the island.

## Cross-city scene design
Use this checklist before turning a profile into a story or quest:
1. **Start with a real need:** repair, food, time, access, safety, information, fairness, or belonging.
2. **Give at least two people reasonable but different priorities.** Avoid defining a whole town by one villain, custom, or belief.
3. **Make local knowledge useful but bounded.** Residents can disagree; a local observation may be outdated or apply only to one circumstance.
4. **Keep evidence visible:** what was observed, when, by whom, and what remains uncertain.
5. **Use magic only when it adds a specific interaction or question.** State who can use it, what it does, what it cannot do, and how non-magical work still matters.
6. **Check the stable place ID and anchor registry.** Do not create a duplicate settlement or silently merge a city with an anchor.
7. **Keep dates and cross-regional links open** unless the timeline, event registry, or Decision Log supports them.

## Questions deliberately left open
- Which settlements are towns, cities, ports, or political centers in final canon?
- Which anchor locations are identical to, near, or distinct from named city candidates?
- What languages, religions, laws, leadership systems, calendars, and formal institutions exist?
- Which goods and people actually travel between regions, by what routes, and how often?
- Where does magic appear in everyday work, and who has the ability or training to interact with it?
- Which scene proposals should become actual stories, and which should remain unused prompts?

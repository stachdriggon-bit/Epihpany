# EPIPHANY — World Map Draft 01: Placement Test

**Status:** First schematic placement test; not a final or scaled map.  
**Map asset:** [EPIPHANY-WORLD-MAP-DRAFT-01.svg](EPIPHANY-WORLD-MAP-DRAFT-01.svg)  
**Branch:** `worldbuilding/lore-foundation`

## Purpose

This draft tests whether the five current geographic regions, thirty approved settlement names, major water bodies, and broad travel corridors can coexist in one legible layout. It is a cartographic planning aid, not a source for exact distances, coordinates, elevation, climate boundaries, or travel times.

## Relative layout used

- **Nirvan** occupies the northern zone, with a broken highland arc and a lower southern transition toward the Sea of Stillness.
- **Ilmar** lies west of the Sea of Dawn. The Ilan drainage trends from western uplands through the basin toward the east-facing coast. Westmere is on the western coast, separate from the Ilan mouth.
- **Orath** lies east of the Sea of Dawn. The Eastern Spine is shown as a broad north-to-south highland system. The lake at Ambrin is schematic; its final water balance and outlet remain open.
- **Tharima** lies south of the central sea. Its wadis are seasonal and not shown as a permanent continent-spanning river.
- **Azmar** is split into Inner Passages, Harbor Islands, and Outer Azmar. It is an archipelago region, not a fifth continent.

### Nirvan — 6/6 placed
- Orven Reach: sheltered harbor; Last Warm Dock is its landmark, not a duplicate harbor.
- Pellisar: lakeside settlement; lake outlet status must be specified.
- Varnholt: forest edge with transport access.

### Azmar — 6/6 placed
- Selka Haven: useful passage harbor; reconcile Seven-Mooring Harbor and Atlas SET-05 Azmar Port into a single canonical location or explicit aliases.
- Tern Isle: navigation-marker island beside a real route.

- Vey Shoals: shoal-fringed channel zone; sediment/current cause remains to be fixed.
- Far Lantern: outer island group; remoteness and water supply need an explicit explanation.

## Findings from the first pass

### Coherent enough to keep
1. The north/west/east/south arrangement reads clearly and supports distinct travel zones.
2. The Sea of Dawn separates Ilmar and Orath while leaving island passages between them.
3. The Sea of Stillness gives Nirvan a southern maritime interface without making all northern waters the same sea.
4. The Strait of Passage remains a regional route rather than the only way to travel between all regions.
5. The three Azmar groups can support several routes and avoid one-harbor/one-bottleneck geography.

### Constraints to resolve before calling the map stable
1. **Ilan River:** settle its source, tributaries, estuary, and the exact upstream crossing. Eiraven and Ilanford must not accidentally become two names for one settlement or two implausibly close crossings.
2. **Orath lake:** decide whether the inland basin is closed, has a seasonal outlet, or drains through a low pass. The map must follow the chosen water balance.
3. **Nirvan lakes:** assign an outlet to Pellisar's lake and a credible catchment/outlet to Mireva's wetland.
4. **Tharima water:** document the water source for Namaris and Kharif separately; preserve Saffra's seasonal flood channel without placing its market in the active floodway.
5. **Azmar freshwater:** confirm Oruven's island is large/wet enough for permanent settlement. If not, move the settlement to a better island and keep Rain-Cistern Island as an independent anchor.
6. **Azmar canonical harbor:** resolve Selka Haven / Seven-Mooring Harbor / Azmar Port as one place with aliases or intentionally distinct locations.
7. **Route continuity:** draw land corridors only where passes, river crossings, and water stops make them credible. No travel times until a scale and terrain model exist.

## Drawing rules for Draft 02

- Refine coastlines first, keeping all five region relationships intact unless an explicit revision is recorded.
- Draw watersheds before final rivers. Do not allow a river to cross the Eastern Spine or a continental divide without a documented pass/outlet explanation.
- Mark major relief and the Orath/Nirvan lake systems before locking city coordinates.
- Treat city dots in Draft 01 as relative anchors only; label collisions and spacing are visual-layout issues, not evidence that the settlements are geographically close.
- Keep the 30 approved names stable even if a settlement's exact location needs adjustment.
- Keep all worldbuilding changes on `worldbuilding/lore-foundation`; do not merge into the application branch as part of this map exercise.

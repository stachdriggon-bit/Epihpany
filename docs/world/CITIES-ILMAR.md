# EPIPHANY — Ilmar: Candidate Cities

**Version:** 0.2  
**Status:** The six city names and the associated village name Willowmere are approved by the project owner. Exact site placement, local details, and some city/anchor relationships remain provisional.
**Scope:** A small first pass at memorable settlements for Ilmar. These names are meant to be tested in stories and exploration before they are finalized.

## Canon decision

**Approved names:** The six names in this registry are accepted as the current canonical names for these settlement IDs. Keep their spelling and Arabic-script forms consistent across stories, quests, cards, dialogue, maps, and translations. A later rename requires an explicit decision and must preserve the stable ID.

**What this does not finalize:** These entries are not a complete gazetteer. Exact positions, physical geography, political importance, and possible overlap with existing anchor locations remain open. If an entry and an anchor prove to be the same place, reconcile them under one stable place record rather than inventing two locations.

## Ground rules

- Ilmar is the western continent in the current working map, but the full world map and all coastlines remain provisional.
- These are the first approved settlement names, not a complete list of settlements.
- The roles below are intentionally distinct; not every place needs a mystery or a grand historical secret.
- Positions are relative descriptions, not exact map coordinates. Do not force rivers, roads, or coastlines to fit a name.
- The continent has no confirmed capital yet. One candidate may later become a capital, but that decision should wait until the political history and map require it.
- Names can be changed. Keep the IDs stable if names change.

## Reusable place registry — check before creating or writing a place

**This file is the reference record for the Ilmar candidates below.** Before adding a city, writing a story set in one, creating a quest, card, dialogue, map label, or localization entry, search this registry and reuse the existing place ID and facts. Do not reintroduce an existing city by copying its description into a new document as if it were a new place. Other documents should link to or reference the place ID and add only story-specific details.

### Continuity rules

1. **Stable IDs beat names.** If a name changes, keep the ID and update this record.
2. **One place, one record.** Alternate names, historic names, districts, and aliases belong in the same record unless the story establishes that they are genuinely separate places.
3. **No duplicate city creation.** Search names, aliases, roles, and IDs before inventing another settlement. Similar functions do not automatically mean two places are the same, but possible overlaps must be checked.
4. **Descriptions are not copy-paste filler.** When another file needs to mention a city, use a short reference to its ID and only the details needed for that scene. Keep the full reusable description here.
5. **Changes must propagate.** If a city changes materially, update this record first, then check the Atlas, anchor locations, stories, quests, character relationships, cards, and translations that reference it.
6. **Keep uncertainty visible.** Proposed rivers, coastlines, routes, capital status, and political affiliations remain provisional until explicitly decided.

## Candidate settlement list

### IL-CITY-01 — Eiraven / إيرافين
**Role:** Major river-market city; possible future capital candidate, not designated as one.  
**Relative position:** On the lower Ilan River, inland from its proposed estuary or near a navigable branch. Exact placement depends on the final river course.  
**Signature:** Busy quays, grain warehouses, ferries, public notice boards, and neighborhoods that grew around different generations of river trade.  
**Everyday concern:** Flood precautions, river access, market rules, and who pays to maintain the banks.  
**Story use:** A lost shipment, a dispute between old riverside families and new merchants, or a public notice whose wording changes the outcome of a quest.  
**Continuity anchor:** Its importance comes from river access and trade, not from an assumed empire or ancient prophecy.

### IL-CITY-02 — Westmere / ويستمير
**Role:** Western coastal port and ship-repair town.  
**Relative position:** On Ilmar's west-facing coast, at a naturally sheltered bay if the final coastline supports one.  
**Signature:** Repair yards, rope-makers, weathered warehouses, and a harbor where local fishing boats share space with long-distance traders.  
**Everyday concern:** Storm damage, harbor fees, and whether scarce timber should go to fishing boats or merchant vessels.  
**Story use:** A crew needs a repair before a seasonal crossing; a worker recognizes a vessel by an unusual repair rather than its flag.  
**Continuity anchor:** The port faces the outer ocean; its connection to central-sea routes depends on a real coastal journey, not a teleport-like shortcut.

### IL-CITY-03 — Talwen / تالْوِن
**Role:** Forest-edge town and timber/craft center.  
**Relative position:** Near the edge of Ilmar's Central Forests, close to paths connecting villages and lowland markets.  
**Signature:** Woodcraft workshops, path markers, small inns, and a market selling practical goods made from forest materials.  
**Everyday concern:** Which paths may be used, how much timber can be cut, and how to keep routes passable after storms.  
**Story use:** A disagreement over a trail, a missing tool that disrupts a whole workshop, or a young guide learning that local knowledge has limits.  
**Continuity anchor:** Forest paths may shift or become overgrown for ordinary reasons; unusual events are possible but not assumed.

### IL-CITY-04 — Ilanford / إيلانفورد
**Role:** River crossing town with a bridge, ferry, and market.  
**Relative position:** At a plausible crossing on the Ilan River or a major tributary, upstream from Eiraven if the final drainage supports that relationship.  
**Signature:** A bridge with visible repair marks, ferry platforms for heavy cargo, and stalls that become busiest on crossing days.  
**Everyday concern:** Bridge maintenance, tolls, flood closures, and competition between ferrymen and bridge operators.  
**Story use:** A local argument about whether crossing should be free; a recurring location that changes as a bridge is built, repaired, bypassed, or abandoned.  
**Continuity anchor:** This candidate overlaps in concept with the Three-Arch Ferry anchor location. They may become the same place; do not treat them as two separate settlements unless a story benefits from that distinction.

### IL-CITY-05 — Calder Vale / وادي كالدر
**Role:** Upland town serving farms, mills, and overland travelers.  
**Relative position:** In a lower valley between Ilmar's western uplands and central lowlands, only if the eventual relief and river network support it.  
**Signature:** Watermills, a compact market square, stone retaining walls, and roads that become difficult after heavy rain.  
**Everyday concern:** Mill rights, road repairs, and balancing local needs against passing merchants' demands.  
**Story use:** A practical engineering problem, a dispute over water use, or a traveler who discovers that the “short road” is only short in good weather.  
**Continuity anchor:** Its water supply and road connections must be checked against the final watershed map.

### IL-CITY-06 — Sorell / سوريل
**Role:** Southern transition-market town linking Ilmar's lowlands to drier southern routes.  
**Relative position:** In Ilmar's southern interior, along a plausible route toward Tharima rather than on an assumed political border.  
**Signature:** Pack-animal yards, mixed produce stalls, inns, and traders who adjust their stock to the season and the direction of travel.  
**Everyday concern:** Seasonal road conditions, fair weights, and agreements between visiting traders and local sellers.  
**Story use:** A misunderstanding over measures or bargaining customs, a delayed caravan, or a character who must ask for directions rather than pretend to know the route.  
**Continuity anchor:** The town is a meeting point, not proof of one unified culture or a fixed border between continents.

## How these candidates relate to the anchor locations

- **The Three-Arch Ferry (IL-LOC-01):** could be the older name or historic district of Ilanford, or remain a separate crossing if the map later supports both.
- **The Lanternwood Edge (IL-LOC-02):** could be a district or nearby settlement associated with Talwen; no final relationship is established.
- **Eiraven and Ilanford:** their river relationship is a proposal only and must be checked against the eventual river course.
- **Westmere:** depends on the final west-coast bay geometry.
- **Calder Vale and Sorell:** their positions are deliberately broad so terrain and routes can be refined without breaking city canon.

- **Known character context:** Veyra grew up here in a small, close-knit family and a quiet village community. This is a character-background reference, not a complete settlement gazetteer.

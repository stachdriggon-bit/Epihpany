# EPIPHANY — Character Ability Register

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Working register; entries may be provisional unless explicitly marked accepted.  
**Purpose:** Keep each character's ability, natural limits, individual restriction, observed test results, and unresolved questions in one stable place so future stories and design sessions do not silently mix hypotheses with canon.

This is a worldbuilding reference only. It does not change app runtime or character-card data.

## 1. Recordkeeping rules

1. Every character uses their stable character ID (for example, `CHR-001`) as the primary reference. Names and titles may change; the ID should not.
2. Never infer magical ability from rarity, narrative importance, species, or appearance. Some characters can use or interact with magic; others cannot.
3. Keep these concepts separate:
   - **Ability/domain:** what the character can do and the kind of operation involved.
   - **Natural limits:** what the ability does not reveal or accomplish, even when functioning normally.
   - **Individual restriction:** a character-specific condition that complicates or alters the operation of their ability.
   - **Knowledge/information:** facts the character does or does not know.
   - **Skill/mastery/adaptation:** how reliably the character uses the ability and strategies they have learned.
   - **Observed outcome:** what actually happened in a particular test or story.
4. A failure is not automatically caused by an individual restriction. Consider ability limits, missing information, environment, target conditions, interpretation, and skill separately.
5. Use explicit status labels:
   - **Accepted:** explicitly approved as current reference by the project owner.
   - **Provisional:** useful working hypothesis; may be revised.
   - **Open:** not decided or insufficiently tested.
   - **Test observation:** result of a specific scenario; not automatically a general rule.
   - **Rejected:** tested and set aside; preserve the reason to avoid repeating the same dead end.
6. Do not promote a test proposal to canon or edit a character card merely because a test seems coherent. Record the result and wait for explicit approval where required. Do not force abilities into the superseded branch taxonomy.
7. If a later test contradicts an earlier one, record the contradiction and compare explanations before rewriting the record.
8. Use the ability-first [Foundational Sorcery Model](MAGIC-FOUNDATIONS.md). Do not treat the retired branch taxonomy as current law; “Perception and Detection” remains an exploratory note, not an official branch.

## 3. Character test record

### CHR-001 — Mira, Wayfinder

- **Record status:** Core restriction accepted; ability's detailed operating parameters remain open.
- **Card rarity:** Epic (existing card decision; unchanged by this test).
- **Magical access:** Open at the level of the wider system; the current test assumes she has a character-specific ability under examination.
- **Ability name:** “Route Thread / خيوط المسارات” — working label only.
- **Core domain:** Hypothesis: mentally perceives spatial relationships between nearby paths and places.
- **Operation:** Hypothesis: senses or mentally recognizes relations such as paths meeting, branching, returning, or sharing a chokepoint. No literal visible threads or complete magical map are assumed.
- **Initial test range:** Nearby spatial relationships only. Long-distance links and non-spatial links remain open.
- **Natural limits under test:**
  - Perceiving a connection does not prove a path is passable or safe.
  - The ability does not automatically reveal a door's state, a bridge's condition, hidden room contents, or a mechanism's function.
  - A path to an unknown place does not reveal what is there.
  - Past traces, future outcomes, causal links, and distant magical links are outside the current test scope.
- **Individual restriction:** Accepted — **Magnitude**. Mira has an intrinsic ceiling on the complexity of spatial relationships she can organize into a reliable mental reading at one time.
- **Restriction trigger(s):** High interconnection can push the network beyond that reliable capacity. An important unknown can also do so when it materially changes the interpretation of the spatial relationships or leaves the network structurally incomplete enough to support competing readings. A minor irrelevant unknown does not trigger the restriction by itself.
- **Severity / combined triggers:** The first effect is impaired confidence calibration: a plausible relationship may feel more certain than the evidence warrants. At higher load, the restriction may interfere with forming the spatial reading itself: Mira may overlook a real relationship, fail to form a clear reading, or temporarily favor a misleading one. The effect is graded, not random or a total shutdown. Exact thresholds remain open.
- **Classification rationale:** Magnitude is the best fit because the essential limit is how much relational complexity Mira can reliably organize simultaneously, not which target types the ability can affect, a forbidden magical act, a mandatory price, or a duration limit.
- **Accepted restriction effect:** Under high relational load, Mira may struggle to distinguish a confirmed relationship from a possible one. In stronger cases, this can also impair the relationships she registers or how she organizes them.
- **Skill and adaptation:** Dividing a complex network into smaller parts, keeping confirmed and possible relationships separate, and comparing the reading with real-world evidence can improve reliability. These strategies reduce simultaneous load but do not remove the intrinsic ceiling; difficulty may return when the parts must be recombined.
- **Important distinction:** Missing information or a mistaken inference is not automatically proof that the restriction activated. Evaluate the ability's natural limits, evidence available, interpretation, environment, and skill separately.
- **Possible functional description:** “Perception and Detection” remains an exploratory description, not an official branch. The restriction category is accepted as Magnitude; this does not establish an official branch taxonomy.
- **Test scenarios discussed:**
  1. Clear nearby paths and shared chokepoints — test premise: the ability should be comparatively reliable when relationships are clear.
  2. A possible connection obscured by mist — test premise: a perceived possibility must remain distinct from a confirmed relationship. A more precise outcome was not separately preserved in the record.
  3. A dense old-city network — test premise: high interconnection may activate the restriction.
  4. A path with an unknown barrier or locked door — tests the distinction between spatial connection and passability; the restriction is not assumed to be the only possible cause of uncertainty.
  5. Dividing a network into smaller parts — tests whether partitioning and checking evidence improve reliability without guaranteeing a complete answer.
  6. Finding a child behind an unfamiliar mechanism — illustrative story test only; the ability does not explain the mechanism itself.
- **Test observations preserved from the story discussion (not canon):**
  - In clearer streets, Mira could recognize spatial relationships more readily.
  - In a densely interconnected alley network, distinguishing reliable relationships from merely possible ones became harder.
  - Recognizing a possible connection did not establish that a locked door or other barrier could be passed.
  - Breaking the network into smaller parts and checking available evidence helped her identify an alternative route, but did not resolve every uncertainty.
  - The ability did not reveal how an unfamiliar locking mechanism worked; Raven and the child provided relevant information.
  - These are observations from an illustrative test scene, not proof of universal rules. The separate mist scenario needs a more precisely recorded result if it is reused.
- **Current test conclusion:** The ability's core spatial-relations domain and the Magnitude restriction are accepted as the current character reference. Exact operating range, thresholds, and remaining ability details are still open. The scenario analysis was not an executed in-game experiment.
- **Accepted restriction model (2026-10-29):** High interconnection and a materially consequential unknown can each increase the effective complexity of the spatial relationship network beyond Mira's reliable simultaneous processing capacity. When both occur together, the load will often be greater, but not by a fixed arithmetic multiplier. Confidence calibration is affected first; in stronger cases, registering or organizing some relationships can also be impaired. The exact threshold remains open.
- **Open questions:**
  - What physical/spatial relationships can be sensed when obstructed from direct view?
  - What exactly does “more severely impaired” look like without making the ability arbitrary?
  - Can the ability recognize its own uncertainty, and under what conditions?
  - How should this ability be distinguished from navigation, detection, or other characters' powers?
  - Would any optional organizational label help once the ability-first framework is tested further?
- **Canon decision:** The user authorized resolving Mira's restriction. The current reference is accepted as **Magnitude**: an intrinsic limit on the amount/complexity of spatial relationships she can reliably organize simultaneously. Exact thresholds and some ability parameters remain open.
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-29 — restriction resolved as Magnitude and marked accepted. Important unknowns matter when they increase effective relational complexity; irrelevant unknowns do not trigger the restriction alone. Exact threshold remains open.


### CHR-002 — Evren, Resonance Tinkerer

- **Record status:** Accepted as the current character reference; some operational parameters remain open.
- **Card rarity:** Uncommon (existing card data; rarity is not a measure of power or narrative importance).
- **Magical access:** Sorcerer; uses the established Aelora energy and Aren Art framework.
- **Core domain:** Resonance patterns and the responses of objects/materials.
- **Operation:** Evren can perceive resonance patterns and magically influence them, including modifying vibrations or changing an object's response within his ability's limits. He must perceive a pattern before influencing it. Touch is not required; contact can improve clarity and control, while distance makes perception and influence harder.
- **Natural limits:** Perception does not automatically reveal a phenomenon's cause, exact measurements, internal structure, or material composition. Similar responses do not prove shared material or structure. Perception does not guarantee that an interpretation or invention will succeed. His practical inventions also depend on knowledge, tools, experimentation, and verification.
- **Individual restriction:** Accepted — **Prohibition**. Evren cannot magically originate or impose a completely new resonance pattern by himself. He can influence and modify a pattern that is already present, but cannot create one from nothing through this ability.
- **Classification rationale:** The essential prohibited act is the magical creation/imposition of a new resonance pattern. This is not a quantitative ceiling on an otherwise available action (Magnitude), nor a restriction on which target categories can be affected (Scope); the act itself is unavailable to him.
- **Restriction and adaptation:** The restriction is intrinsic and present from the appearance of his restraint symbol. Training can improve perception and modification of existing patterns, but cannot make the prohibited act available. No additional effects or numerical thresholds are established.
- **Backstory context:** His restraint symbol appeared when he was five, an exceptionally early and rare age. The timing was unusual, not a prophecy or hidden selection. A sorcerer noticed the phenomenon, took him along, and helped him understand Aelora energy and Aren Art. This experience shaped the curiosity that later became central to his character.
- **Character context:** His personal motivation is exploration and understanding. His practical purpose is to turn discoveries into tools and solutions that help people. He is curious, observant, practical, inventive, open, friendly and persistent; he may become absorbed in discoveries, experiment too quickly, struggle to leave questions unresolved, or underestimate risks when excited.
- **Conditions for use:** Exact maximum distance, target count, material coverage, perceptual presentation, and any special Aelora cost remain open. Contact improves clarity/control but is not required; greater distance increases difficulty.
- **Age and place:** 28 years old; from Dornwick / دورنويك, Orath (OR-CITY-05). Current residence and exact calendar birth date remain open.
- **Era:** The current era, identified by the project owner as the latest era in the world's era division; use the finalized era register's exact title when that register is reconciled. He shares this era with Mira and Veyra.
- **Relationships:** The sorcerer who introduced him to the magical world has scholarly standing and field/political responsibilities within a political institution. Identity, institution, and exact duties are open. No specific relationship with Mira or Veyra is established.
- **Observed results:** No in-game experiment or canonical story event has been recorded. The points above are the accepted character reference, not test results.
- **Open questions:** Exact operating range and target coverage; perceptual presentation and costs, if any; identity and institution of the sorcerer who guided him; current residence; species/nature; exact calendar birth date; future named relationships and story events.
- **Canon decision:** On 2026-10-30, the user approved resolving Evren's individual restriction and completing his foundational character file. The selected restriction is Prohibition: he cannot originate a new resonance pattern magically, but can modify an existing one.
- **Reference card:** [Evren — CHR-002](../character-reference-cards/CHR-002-IDRIS.md)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-30 — name, core personality, motivation, early sorcerer background, resonance ability, Prohibition restriction, age, homeland, current-era placement, and signature quote recorded.

### CHR-039 — Veyra, Pattern Detective

- **Record status:** Accepted as the current reference concept; operational details remain open.
- **Card rarity:** Common (existing card data; unchanged and not a measure of ability strength).
- **Magical access:** Uses magic within the established Aelora Energy / Aren / Aren Art framework.
- **Ability name:** “Pattern Perception / إدراك الأنماط” — provisional descriptive label, not a final magical name.
- **Core domain:** Relations and patterns among accessible evidence, traces, objects, and events.
- **Operation:** Veyra uses a magical art to perceive relationships that may be difficult to assemble from isolated evidence. The perception supports hypotheses and investigation; it does not provide guaranteed explanations.
- **Natural limits:**
  - A perceived relation does not prove its cause or interpretation.
  - The ability does not automatically reveal a person's identity, motives, thoughts, honesty, or the exact time of an event.
  - It cannot retrieve information from an event that left no accessible evidence.
  - A pattern may be coincidental or deliberately arranged to mislead.
  - Perceiving magical traces does not automatically explain an unfamiliar magical mechanism or grant control over it.
- **Individual restriction:** Accepted as the current reference concept — **Scope**. Veyra has an intrinsic limit on the breadth of the relationship network she can keep in a coherent, reliable analysis at one time.
- **Restriction effects under the current model:** When the relationships she tries to combine exceed that scope, some connections may no longer be available together, or she may be unable to integrate the evidence into one reliable picture. The exact operational variables and thresholds remain open.
- **Classification rationale:** Scope is the selected fit because the proposed intrinsic limit concerns the breadth of relations she can analyze together. The precise definition must remain consistent with the project's restraint-category principles; no numerical threshold is established.
- **Skill and adaptation:** Breaking an investigation into smaller groups, recording confirmed links separately from hypotheses, and comparing partial conclusions can improve practical reliability. These strategies do not remove the intrinsic limit; difficulty may return when groups must be recombined.
- **Conditions for use:** Access/proximity requirements, whether sight alone is sufficient, deliberate activation, sensory presentation, any special Aelora cost, and any visible mark remain open. Do not invent them.
- **Test scenarios discussed (hypothetical, not in-world observations):**
  1. Genuine evidence arranged to mislead an investigator — Veyra may notice inconsistencies but is not a truth detector and can still adopt a false hypothesis.
  2. A decisive missing clue — she may identify competing explanations but cannot choose the truth without differentiating evidence; missing information alone is not proof of restriction activation.
  3. A highly complex network of relationships — the principal scenario for testing Scope, as simultaneous integration may exceed the reliable scope.
  4. An event leaving no accessible trace — outside the ability's natural information boundary.
  5. Training and experience — improve evidence selection and hypothesis checking but do not erase Scope.
  6. An unfamiliar magical trace — relationships may be perceived without automatically understanding the underlying magic.
- **Observed results:** No in-game experiment or canonical story event has been recorded. The cases above are design tests only.
- **Open questions:** Final magical ability name; access and range conditions; whether deliberate activation is required; perceptual presentation; Aelora cost, if any; exact factors that define Scope; visible mark, if any; age, species/nature, backstory, motives, relationships, and distinctive dialogue.
- **Canon decision:** On 2026-10-29, the user approved the Veyra reference concept and the Scope restriction for the character file. This accepts the current ability/restriction description as the project reference while leaving the listed operating details open.
- **Reference card:** [Veyra — CHR-039](../character-reference-cards/CHR-039-VEYRA.md)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-29 — ability and Scope reference accepted; operational details remain open.

### CHR-005 — Lucian, Crystal Echo

- **Record status:** Foundational character and ability concept accepted on 2026-10-31; domain-event mysteries and detailed operating parameters remain open.
- **Card rarity:** Epic (card-system classification only; not a measure of strength or narrative importance).
- **Age and homeland:** 25 years old; from Urven Reach / أورفن ريتش, Nirvan / نيرفان.
- **Nature:** Human with crystalline traits; can assume a partial crystalline form while retaining his human appearance and can use his ability without transforming.
- **Former organization:** Aegis Concord, an independent international field-protection organization headquartered in Urven Reach. Its work concerns protecting inhabited areas, routes, convoys and expeditions from domain-related dangers. Internal structure and ranks remain open.
- **Core ability — Echo Response:** The crystalline structure in Lucian's body responds to residual traces left by interactions and phenomena in its surroundings.
- **Echo categories:** Magical traces (spell use, Aelora interactions and disturbances left by sorcerers' abilities) and physical traces (vibrations, pressure, impacts, collisions, collapses and strong physical changes). Domain phenomena may leave either or both kinds; they are not a separate third category.
- **Operation:** Sensing an echo, attuning the crystalline structure to its pattern, then responding according to the nature of the trace.
- **Possible applications:** Responding to resonance and structural changes; sensing some changes in objects, cavities and disturbances; adjusting crystalline cohesion or distribution to resist some effects and reduce impacts; limited attunement to existing magical traces; and partial adaptation to unfamiliar domain conditions.
- **Natural limits:** Lucian cannot create echoes from nothing, copy spells or abilities, read object memories or view the past, automatically identify an echo's source, or exert absolute control over materials and phenomena. Old, weak or overlapping traces are harder to process; attunement can fail. Strong or prolonged use consumes Aelora energy and may exhaust him. The ability does not grant immunity to domains.
- **Combat role:** Echo Response is not inherently a direct combat ability. Lucian's field experience helps him apply it defensively to a limited extent; it does not make him an overwhelmingly powerful fighter.
- **Former sorcery and restraint mystery:** Lucian was a sorcerer before the domain incident and had a magical ability, restraint symbol and individual restriction. During his time inside the domain, his former magic, symbol and restriction disappeared. It remains unresolved whether they were permanently lost or access to them was severed. Do not infer that the absence of the former symbol/restriction makes him stronger than other sorcerers. Keep this mystery out of the player-facing card summary.
- **Backstory context:** His family was of moderate means before an unresolved event worsened their circumstances. From childhood he struggled to gather and control his power, contributing to his accumulated experience. He accepted Aegis Concord field-protection work partly because of the good pay and his need to earn money.
- **Domain incident:** He was trapped for a long time inside an unusual or powerful domain. Explorers mistook his crystalline appearance for that of a guardian and called him Crystal Echo. Another sorcerer eventually approached and helped him escape. The domain's nature and the incident's cause remain unresolved; do not assume a connection to Numira.
- **Travel and relationships:** Lucian did not originally enjoy travelling or exploration; work and financial circumstances made travel familiar. His current exploration is encouraged by a friend who accompanies him. He values the rescue but does not want permanent indebtedness; the friendship develops into genuine companionship. The rescuer and current travel companion are not yet confirmed to be the same person.
- **Open questions:** Family event; early training details; exact domain and cause/duration of confinement; relation, if any, to Numira; whether former magic/symbol/restriction are gone or inaccessible; precise crystalline-form and Echo Response parameters; identity of rescuer and travel companion; Aegis Concord ranks and structure; current residence; signature quote and further relationships.
- **Canon decision:** On 2026-10-31, the user approved the name Lucian / لوسيان instead of the former card name Nibras / نبراس, age 25, Crystal Echo title, human nature with crystalline traits, Urven Reach in Nirvan as homeland and Aegis Concord as his former organization. The core Echo Response concept and its boundaries, personality, work background, domain incident and travel direction were accepted for the character reference.

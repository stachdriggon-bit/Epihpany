# EPIPHANY — Peoples and Species

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Foundational framework; only explicitly recorded traits below are accepted. Detailed biology, cultures, and histories remain open.

## Design principles

- A people or species is not a single culture, state, personality, or lifestyle.
- A shared innate trait does not determine an individual's profession, beliefs, values, or choices.
- Distinguish biological or innate characteristics from cultural practices, learned skills, and individual abilities.
- Aelora Energy may express different characteristics among different peoples. Do not assume every member has identical access, capacity, skill, or magical ability.
- Do not infer that ancestry automatically grants sorcery or a specific personal ability.
- Record hypotheses and open questions separately from accepted traits.

## Elves

**Status:** Accepted foundational direction; further details open.

### Distinctive affinity

Elves have an exceptional innate sensitivity to and understanding of the places and environments around them. This is a characteristic of their people, not a claim that every Elf has identical knowledge or is incapable of misunderstanding a place.

The exact range, limits, and practical expressions of this sensitivity remain open. It should not automatically be treated as omniscience, remote detection, control over nature, or a supernatural ability to communicate with every living thing.

### Aelora Energy

Differences in the characteristics or expression of Aelora Energy between peoples are an intended part of the setting's distinctions between them. The particular Elven expression, its mechanisms, and whether it is universal among Elves remain undefined.

Do not invent specific powers, costs, or techniques from this premise alone.

### Principal home

**Eiraven / إيرافين (IL-CITY-01)** in Ilmar is the principal home city of the Elves.

- The city lies within a broad landscape of extensive forests, lakes, valleys, and waterways.
- Its built environment is integrated with and intended to coexist with the surrounding natural environment.
- Eiraven is a principal center, not the only place where Elves live.
- This does not mean all Elves live in forests, share one culture, or follow one political authority.

See [Ilmar Settlement Registry](CITIES-ILMAR.md) for the single authoritative city record.

### Open questions

- The exact limits and everyday expressions of Elven environmental sensitivity.
- The specific relationship between Elves and Aelora Energy.
- Elven lifespans, physical variation, languages, family structures, customs, political organization, and historical migrations.
- Other Elf settlements and the diversity of Elven communities.
- Whether the innate sensitivity is experienced identically by all Elves or varies between individuals.

Do not close these questions without discussion and explicit approval.

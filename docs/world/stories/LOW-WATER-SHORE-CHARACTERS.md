# Pilot Character Records — The Low-Water Shore

**Status:** Provisional story-specific character canon for editorial use. These records establish the characters only for this proposed story arc; they do not make the story or location's unresolved history final canon.  
**Region:** Orath (working map).  
**Place reference:** OR-LOC-02 — The Low-Water Shore. Do not use Ambrin as a separate location while its registry relationship remains unresolved.

## OR-CHAR-001 — Tarin Vell

- **Name:** Tarin Vell
- **Role:** Boat repairer and practical user of the landing area.
- **Age/lifespan:** Adult; exact age intentionally unspecified until needed by a story.
- **Visual direction:** Work-worn clothes suitable for lakeside repairs; practical tool roll; hands marked by routine craft work. Avoid a “mysterious chosen one” silhouette.
- **Personality:** Direct, observant, and protective of other people's time. He prefers decisions that can be acted on today.
- **Contradiction/flaw:** His practicality can make him impatient with records and slow investigations; when a decision affects safety or livelihoods, he is willing to pause.
- **Independent motivation:** Keep the landing usable and protect the local repair trade through changing water levels.
- **Strengths:** Knows boats, landing steps, work routines, and the immediate practical consequences of a high or low shoreline.
- **Limits:** His experience is local and hands-on. He cannot infer a long-term water trend from a few seasons or determine why the lake changes.
- **Backstory:** Grew up around the landing and learned repair work from local craftspeople. Specific family history, apprenticeships, and past conflicts remain open until the plot needs them.
- **Relationships:** Works with Sella Orin because her records can inform practical choices. They respect each other's competence but differ in pace; no romance, kinship, or prior betrayal is implied.
- **Distinctive phrase:** “Useful is not the same as certain.”  
  **Arabic:** «ما يفيدنا عمليًا ليس بالضرورة أمرًا مؤكّدًا».
- **Knowledge boundaries:** Knows what he has personally seen at the landing and how work is affected. He may suspect a pattern but must label it as a suspicion, not a proven cause.
- **First appearance:** The Two Records at the Low-Water Shore, Episode 1 (draft).
- **Story-use tags:** practical decision-making, craft knowledge, observation versus inference, local livelihood.
- **Open threads:** Whether he will learn to tolerate longer investigations; the lake's long-term trend remains unresolved.

## OR-CHAR-002 — Sella Orin

- **Name:** Sella Orin
- **Role:** Local record keeper who maintains and compares working records.
- **Age/lifespan:** Adult; exact age intentionally unspecified until needed by a story.
- **Visual direction:** Practical layered clothing, a weather-protected notebook case, ink or graphite marks from daily record work. Her design should read as a working professional, not a secretive fantasy oracle.
- **Personality:** Careful, patient, and attentive to how information was gathered.
- **Contradiction/flaw:** She can focus so closely on the limits of a record that she delays a decision even when a temporary, reversible action would help.
- **Independent motivation:** Preserve useful local records and make sure decisions do not mistake incomplete evidence for certainty.
- **Strengths:** Compares sources, asks who recorded something and for what purpose, and notices missing dates or inconsistent methods.
- **Limits:** She cannot make a weak record reliable by interpreting it cleverly. She was not present for every event and does not know the lake's cause.
- **Backstory:** Maintains practical records used by residents and workers. The institution, funding, family background, and training behind her role remain open; do not invent an archive or ancient order without a story need.
- **Relationships:** Collaborates with Tarin on the landing records. She values his direct observations; he values her comparisons, though each sometimes finds the other's method frustrating.
- **Distinctive phrase:** “First, let us ask what the record actually measured.”  
  **Arabic:** «أولًا، لنسأل عمّا قاسه السجل فعلًا».
- **Knowledge boundaries:** Knows the stated purpose and visible contents of the records introduced in the story. She does not know who is right until the evidence supports a conclusion.
- **First appearance:** The Two Records at the Low-Water Shore, Episode 1 (draft).
- **Story-use tags:** source evaluation, cautious inference, records and memory, evidence limits.
- **Open threads:** How she balances careful documentation with timely action; the lake's long-term trend remains unresolved.

## Relationship and continuity rules

1. Tarin is not “the practical one who is always right,” and Sella is not “the scholar who is always right.” Their methods reveal different kinds of evidence and different blind spots.
2. Neither character should assume fraud, a cover-up, a supernatural cause, or an ancient civilization without specific evidence.
3. They can make mistakes, but the story must show why a conclusion seemed reasonable and what evidence changes it.
4. Neither character should know information that has not reached them through direct observation, records, conversation, or a plausible route.
5. Their relationship is professional collaboration and mutual respect with ordinary friction. Any stronger relationship requires a deliberate later decision.
6. These IDs are story-character IDs, not collectible-card IDs. Do not create cards or card-upgrade revelations for them until their recurring role and visual design are approved.
7. Names are provisional to the pilot. If changed, preserve the IDs and update all episodes and translations together.

## 2. Character overview
Riven is a capable, dependable young explorer. Despite his age, he handles danger well and is comfortable dealing with people. He values independence and enjoys exploration for its own sake, but his defining pursuit is tied to a question his grandfather shared with him from childhood.

## 3. Personality
- Independent; he follows his own path and is not defined by being another character's companion.

- Talented and reliable despite his youth.
- Good at handling dangerous situations and interacting with people.
- His social confidence may relate to his wealthy family background, but this explanation is not established.

## 4. Abilities and skills

### Ri Vision — accepted name
A vision-based magical ability with two connected aspects:

**Structural Perception**
- Perceives details of the underlying structures of objects and beings.
- Can notice irregularities, abnormalities and potential weak points.
- Does not automatically reveal every hidden fact or explain what a structure means.

**Kinetic Perception**
- Notices the beginnings of movement and subtle changes.
- Helps analyse attack directions and respond quickly.
- Does not grant foresight or show the future.

**Applications**
- Combat and defence.
- Exploration and noticing environmental or domain irregularities.
- Reading structural details and movement.
- Recognizing that Lucian's crystalline appearance is not an independent creature.

**Information boundary:** What Riven perceives still requires interpretation. His reading can be incomplete or mistaken; the ability does not reveal the complete truth automatically.

## 5. Individual restriction

- **Category:** Continuity / الاستمرارية
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Working description:** Continuous perception.
- Ri Vision's active magical reading depends on Riven maintaining visual tracking and attention on the target being analysed.
- If visual tracking is interrupted, or he shifts his attention away, the accumulated magical analysis loses precision.
- When he resumes tracking, he must rebuild the active reading.
- The restriction affects the magical analysis, not ordinary memory. Riven does not forget details he previously saw with his normal senses.
- Tactical consequences include vulnerability to obstacles, sudden changes of position, multiple targets, and situations that force him to monitor several things at once.

**Classification rationale:** The restriction limits the continuation of the active perceptual operation, rather than the amount or intensity of its effect. This makes Continuity the accepted category.

**Open operational details:** Exact reset behaviour, how partial attention affects the reading, whether all targets behave identically, and any further edge cases. Do not add a numerical duration or energy cost without a separate decision.

## 7. Family and backstory

Riven comes from a wealthy family with a registered magical heritage. The family's ordinary wealth is distinct from the magical legacy, which includes artifacts and may include installations or locations. These registered assets require a recognized magical authority to control them.

### Grandfather and grandmother
Riven's grandfather was deeply attached to a magical heirloom associated with his late wife. He could not bear to transfer that part of the family heritage to someone else before achieving his personal goal concerning it. This attachment was the principal reason he delayed transferring the family's magical authority to Riven's father; lack of trust or the father's readiness was not the main reason.

The grandfather was relatively withdrawn from the wider family because of his preoccupation. He found in young Riven someone to whom he could speak openly about his private research. Riven was fascinated by the questions and possibilities, though he was too young when his grandmother died to understand the grief behind the grandfather's fixation.

The exact nature of the heirloom and what it preserves from the grandmother remain open. The grandfather did not necessarily seek a complete resurrection; even something that recalled her could matter to him.

### Raw Aelora hypothesis
The grandfather wondered whether direct control of raw Aelora—distinct from ordinary magic that uses Aelora to produce effects—could be applied to an artifact holding an Aelora trace of a deceased person. In theory, this might go beyond preserving an echo and perhaps make restoration of life possible.

This is strictly theoretical:
- No successful return from death is known or established.
- The hypothesis is not a proven magical law.
- The nature and sufficiency of any retained trace are unknown.
- Whether restoration is possible at all remains unknown.

### The legendary figure
The grandfather searched for a legendary figure from another, long-lived non-human race who disappeared inside a domain. The figure is known for an achievement independent of domains: reaching a state of direct control over raw Aelora that others consider impossible under the accepted understanding of magical laws.

- The achievement is a state or condition, not a one-time action.
- It is theoretically learnable by any mage; it is not an innate exclusive ability.
- Only this figure is currently known to have reached it.
- Others studied and attempted to reproduce it without success.
- The figure's identity, race, name, exact achievement mechanics and evidence in the manuscript remain open.

### Grandfather's condition and the family legacy
The grandfather later entered an unconscious, coma-like condition. His body remains alive while raw Aelora is steadily drained from him, as if something were using him as a source. The condition is associated with a known natural or cosmic phenomenon believed to be unsurvivable.

The condition's name, mechanism, cause and geography remain open. The exact relationship between the phenomenon and the raw-Aelora hypothesis is not settled.

Because the grandfather cannot complete the legally recognized transfer of authority, the family's stewardship of its registered magical heritage is at risk under public-safety rules for artifacts without an authorized controller. The law is not designed to target this family, and ordinary wealth is not subject to seizure. Exact transfer procedures and deadlines remain open.

## 8. Story motivation and role
Riven's pursuit connects:
1. Saving his grandfather.
2. Continuing the research that fascinated him since childhood.
3. Finding the legendary figure or reliable knowledge of the state they reached.
4. Testing whether direct control of raw Aelora can interrupt the depletion affecting his grandfather.
5. Protecting the family's magical heritage.

These are connected parts of one pursuit, not unrelated goals. The result of the raw-Aelora hypothesis is not guaranteed.

A central theme is: **When is persistence an expression of love, and when does love make someone lose sight of limits?** Riven may come to understand his grandfather's drive through his own experiences, including his developing relationship with Lucian. Do not predetermine the events or outcome.

## 9. Relationships
- **Grandfather:** Shared his private research with Riven from childhood. Their bond is a major emotional foundation of Riven's story.
- **Grandmother:** Died when Riven was too young to understand the loss. A magical heirloom associated with her became central to the grandfather's pursuit.
- **Father:** Intended successor to the family's magical authority. Further family dynamics remain open.
- **Lucian (CHR-005):** They encountered each other by chance while following independent paths inside a domain. Riven did not enter to rescue Lucian. Their relationship and development remain open; Riven must not be reduced to Lucian's rescuer or companion.

## 10. Homeland — Veyrun / فيرون (OR-CITY-01)
Veyrun is an old, prosperous terraced highland city in Orath, known for stonework and water management. Its existing identity—terraced neighbourhoods, steep slopes, runoff and shared water channels—is preserved.

The city's ancient and wealthy character is accepted. Its broader history, sources of wealth, political structure, prominent families and exact geography remain open. Riven's family is wealthy and holds a significant magical heritage, but it is not established as the city's ruling or most powerful family.

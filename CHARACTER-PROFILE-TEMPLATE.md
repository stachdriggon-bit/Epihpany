# EPIPHANY Character Profile Template

**Status:** Approved foundation for all character profiles and the Character Collection.  
**Purpose:** Keep every character's information consistent, readable, expandable, and grounded in documented canon.

This document defines the content structure and presentation rules. It does not itself change the current Collection UI or rewrite existing character records.

## Core presentation

- Use one consistent profile layout for every character.
- Desktop: the character's illustrated card occupies the left column; the information panel occupies the right column and can scroll independently.
- Mobile: show the character card first, followed by the information.
- Prevent overlap between the card and information panel at every screen size.
- Use a single language toggle (English ↔ Arabic). It replaces text in the same location; it must not duplicate translated text beneath the original. Support correct RTL layout for Arabic.
- Keep the character's **name** as the visible profile heading. Do not display the internal character/card ID to players.
- Internal stable IDs may still be retained in data for references, relationships, and system organization.
- Rarity may influence the card frame, accents, and decorative details, but must not change the information structure or imply narrative importance.
- Use restrained fantasy-inspired typography and ornamentation. Keep the information panel clear and easy to scan.
- Information sections should be collapsible to keep long profiles manageable. Keep the identity and overview easy to find.
- On incomplete profiles, use **Not documented yet** (and its Arabic equivalent) where appropriate. Never invent missing facts.

## Fixed information order

The order below is shared by every character. Do not reorder sections to compensate for missing information.

### 1. Identity
A concise identity block near the top of the information panel:
- Character name and known titles/epithets.
- Age, or a clear description of the character's nature/life stage when ordinary age does not apply.
- Species or being type, when documented.
- Homeland / place of origin.
- Current residence or current location, if known.
- Role, occupation, or defining status.

Keep origin and current location distinct. Do not assume they are the same.

### 2. Overview
A short introduction that communicates who the character is without replacing the detailed sections below.

### 3. Personality
Documented temperament, behavior, values, habits, and ways of thinking.

### 4. Motivation
Goals, desires, fears, needs, and the reasons behind the character's choices, when known.

### 5. Abilities & Skills
Abilities, powers, knowledge, talents, and practical skills. Include conditions of use where documented.

### 6. Weaknesses & Limits
Explicit weaknesses, costs, restrictions, vulnerabilities, and boundaries. Do not invent balancing weaknesses.

### 7. Backstory
Known history, formative events, past experiences, and confirmed secrets. Distinguish established facts from rumors or conflicting accounts.

### 8. Story Role
The character's role in stories, conflicts, quests, and wider events. Narrative importance is independent of card rarity.

### 9. Relationships
Documented connections with other characters, including the nature and current state of each relationship when known.

### 10. Places
Places meaningfully connected to the character, such as:
- Places of origin and former homes.
- Current residence or whereabouts.
- Places visited or lived in.
- Locations connected to important events.

Link places to the world/archive collection when that system supports it. Do not duplicate the concise origin/current-location fields from Identity unnecessarily; use this section for context and history.

### 11. Signature Quote
A distinctive line spoken by or strongly associated with the character, shown in English with a natural Arabic translation when available. Do not fabricate a quote.

## Canon and data rules

- Preserve all previously documented information when migrating existing profiles into this template.
- Do not fill gaps with guesses. Mark unknown or undocumented details clearly.
- Distinguish confirmed facts, in-world rumors, disputed accounts, and unknown information whenever the source material makes that distinction.
- Age, species/nature, origin, and current location must not be inferred from artwork alone.
- Character rarity is a collection/game attribute, not a measure of a character's importance, power, or story relevance.
- Internal IDs remain stable even though they are hidden from the player-facing profile.
- Future card upgrades may reveal additional documented information, memories, or secrets. They should expand the profile without silently rewriting established identity or contradicting canon.
- Character tags may be maintained as internal metadata for future story, quest, and World-game use; they are not a mandatory visible section in this profile order.

## Migration checklist

When converting an existing character:
1. Gather every existing documented fact before changing its structure.
2. Map each fact to the appropriate section above.
3. Preserve exact names, relationships, quotes, limitations, and place references.
4. Flag missing or ambiguous information instead of resolving it by invention.
5. Check English and Arabic content for equivalent meaning.
6. Verify the profile follows the fixed section order and responsive presentation rules.
